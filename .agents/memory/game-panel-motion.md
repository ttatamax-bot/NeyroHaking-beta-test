---
name: Game panel motion
description: Visual direction for Memory and Concentration gameplay panels after rejecting orbital backgrounds.
---

Gameplay panels should be opaque instrument surfaces, not transparent windows over decorative rings. Keep the matrix, symbols, and signal shapes only when they are functional parts of the exercise; do not use a decorative grid texture inside the card. Status icons and next-level controls should sit directly on the panel without their own mini-cards; success uses a full orange transition state with a large white looping medal icon and a borderless right arrow.

**Why:** On 2026-08-19 the user rejected the orbital background and visible card grid because they made the gameplay surfaces feel transparent and generic instead of intentional.

**How to apply:** Put motion inside the interaction: staggered challenge reveal, animated preparation/timer cues, tactile press/hover feedback, target scanning, and distinct success/failure transitions. Keep global gameplay backdrops free of breathing blur; use sparse vertical dashed instrument rings instead. Reuse the PotentialScale technical-panel language rather than cosmic/orbital decoration.
