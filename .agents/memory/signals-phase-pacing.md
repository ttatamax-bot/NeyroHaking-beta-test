---
name: Signals phase pacing
description: The concentration Signals practice needs a deliberate preparation and result cadence.
---

Signals must begin with a visible preparation phase lasting at least three seconds. After every successful target tap, show the measured result for 1.5 seconds before the user-controlled next-level transition. A distractor signal must remain visible for at least 1.5 seconds before entering preparation. Early levels use a single red distractor color; additional colors are introduced later, with yellow reserved for level 10.

**Why:** Immediate target presentation made the exercise feel rushed and caused users to miss the mechanic before they could understand what was happening.

**How to apply:** Keep preparation, signal result, success, and failure as explicit phases driven by timers or explicit user actions, and unlock/resume Web Audio from the start gesture so mobile browsers do not drop the first cue.
