---
name: Signals phase pacing
description: The concentration Signals practice needs a deliberate preparation and result cadence.
---

Signals must begin with a visible preparation phase lasting at least three seconds. After every successful target tap, show the measured result for 1.5 seconds before entering the next preparation phase; phase transitions should have distinct sounds.

**Why:** Immediate target presentation made the exercise feel rushed and caused users to miss the mechanic before they could understand what was happening.

**How to apply:** Keep preparation and result as explicit game phases driven by timers, and unlock/resume Web Audio from the start gesture so mobile browsers do not drop the first cue.
