---
name: Article unlock conditions
description: The first articles unlock from first-time behavior instead of key purchases.
---

A2 unlocks after any recorded meditation, A3 after the user has created at least one goal, and A4 after any recorded visualization. A5 and A6 remain key purchases at 400 keys each.

**Why:** The product removed separate key charges for the first technique-linked articles and wants new users to progress through the intended behavior sequence.

**How to apply:** Derive access from hydrated activity/goals as well as persisted `unlockedArticles`, so a first completion unlocks the article across devices without requiring a separate purchase transaction.
